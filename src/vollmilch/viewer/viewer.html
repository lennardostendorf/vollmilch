    vollmilch viewer

    Gating. Choose X and Y, pick Rectangle gate or Polygon gate, and draw. A new gate is a child of the population currently shown (breadcrumb, top right). Click a population on the right to show its cells and gate further inside it. Rectangle edges dragged onto the plot border are open-ended.

    Gates on UMAP axes work, but only cells that were part of the embedding have UMAP coordinates.

    Cells. In Select mode click a point to inspect it or drag a box to show the cells in it (only cells with stored thumbnails can be shown). Click a tile to highlight that cell in the plot.

    Thumbnails. Switch channels on/off, choose colours and set the black (left) and white (right) points. Outlines show the nucleus and the cell of the centred object.

    Saving. Gates are stored in this browser automatically. Save gates downloads a JSON that you can share, load into another copy of this report, or apply to all cells and send to QuPath:

    vollmilch export config.yaml gates.json
