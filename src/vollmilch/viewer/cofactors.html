Every marker is shown as asinh(intensity / cofactor), one ridge per sample. Values well below the cofactor are transformed nearly linearly and squeezed towards 0; values well above it are log-scaled. A good cofactor keeps the background (negative cells) as one compact peak near 0 and spreads the positive cells out to its right. The dashed line is intensity = cofactor.

Suggested = median over samples of each sample's suggestion (▲ on its ridge), which sits at the upper edge of the negative cells. With a measurable background: background mode + × background SD. Background-subtracted (background set to exactly 0): the split between cells with only a few stray positive pixels and positive cells, which form two humps on the log scale. Set cofactors before gating: the gates are drawn on these axes and shift when a cofactor changes.
